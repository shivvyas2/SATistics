# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SATistics to be a welcoming project for everyone, including students who are contributing to open source for the first time. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and constructive, especially with newcomers
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback on code and ideas
- Assuming good intent and asking questions before drawing conclusions
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use SATistics

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and any public space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through the contact options on [shivvyas.com](https://www.shivvyas.com) or a private message to [@shivvyas2](https://github.com/shivvyas2) on GitHub. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful.
